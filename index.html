THE IDEA

OVERTURE'S MISSION

In a perfect world, great music is a shared resource.

Instruments can change the way you think, play, and create. Access to them shouldn't depend on what you can afford.

Overture Instruments is an independent project building detailed, MIDI-playable emulators and creative tools for curious musicians everywhere. The software is free, the code is public, and the point is simple: to make music more open to everyone.

A BRAND-NEW KIND OF WORKSPACE

Introducing
MNI

What if a keyboard could be built around the way you want to make music? With Overture OMNI, you can do just that.

Overture's Open-Source Modular Network Instrument allows you to synthesize sound from its core components: oscillators, noise, filters, envelopes, LFOs, amplifiers, sequencers and instrument-derived blocks such as the Moog ladder filter or the Roland Juno chorus. Connect real audio and control signals, map your MIDI conroller, play the result, then save it, export it as JSON, or draw it as an SVG schematic.

Down the line, I have plans to support native CAD export; the schematics are my first step in that direction.

DISCOVER THE SCIENCE OF SOUND

Understand sound
with Experiments

Overture Experiments are small, hands-on simulations for the curious, allowing you to explore oscillators, filters, envelopes, modulation, and the building blocks of synthesis by changing things and hearing what happens. These PhET-style mini-programs demonstrate the exact same principles that power the Overture emulators, and they are ideal for both classroom-style education and for self-directed learning.

No musical experience required.
