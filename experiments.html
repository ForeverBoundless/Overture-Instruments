LEARN BY PLAYING

Understand sound
by exploring it.

Four small laboratories in the spirit of PhET simulations. Each one plays real audio in your browser, draws what the signal is actually doing, and asks you to change one thing at a time and listen. No musical experience, MIDI controller or sign-up required.

EXPERIMENT 001

Oscillator Lab

Where sound starts. Choose a waveform, set its frequency and amplitude, and watch the measured waveform and spectrum change as you listen.

  • sine, triangle, square, sawtooth
  • frequency ↔ musical pitch
  • why shapes sound different
OPEN LAB → EXPERIMENT 002

Filter and Resonance Lab

Carve frequencies away from a sound with low-, high- and band-pass filters, push the resonance, and sweep the cutoff with an LFO or an envelope.

  • theoretical response curve + measured spectrum
  • cutoff, resonance, bypass comparison
  • auto-wah and plucks
OPEN LAB → EXPERIMENT 003

Envelope Lab

Hold a key and release it while a marker travels through attack, decay, sustain and release on a curve drawn from the same numbers that shape the sound.

  • sustain is a level, release is a time
  • pluck vs. pad vs. organ presets
  • live stage indicator
OPEN LAB → EXPERIMENT 004

FM Synthesis Lab

Two operators, one idea: let a modulator wobble a carrier's pitch at audio rate and hear bells, brass and metal appear as sidebands in the spectrum.

  • carrier, modulator, ratio, depth
  • fixed vs. ratio modulators
  • the principle behind the DX7
OPEN LAB →

Honest visualisation: plots marked "measured" come from an analyser on the audio you hear; plots marked "theoretical" are computed from the control settings. Audio only starts when you press Play or a key. Space bar toggles play, Escape stops everything.
