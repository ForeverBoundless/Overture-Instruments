LEARN BY PLAYING

Understand sound
with Experiments

Here is Overture's collection of small laboratories in the spirit of PhET simulations. Each one plays real audio in your browser, draws what the signal is actually doing, and asks you to change one thing at a time and listen. No musical experience, MIDI controller or sign-up required.

EXPERIMENT 001

Oscillator Lab

Where sound starts. Choose a waveform, set its frequency and amplitude, and watch the measured waveform and spectrum change as you listen.

  • sine, triangle, square, sawtooth
  • frequency ↔ musical pitch
  • why shapes sound different
OPEN LAB → EXPERIMENT 002

Filter and Resonance Lab

Carve frequencies away from a sound with low-, high- and band-pass filters, push the resonance, and sweep the cutoff with an LFO or an envelope.

  • theoretical response curve + measured spectrum
  • cutoff, resonance, bypass comparison
  • auto-wah and plucks
OPEN LAB → EXPERIMENT 003

Envelope Lab

Hold a key and release it while a marker travels through attack, decay, sustain and release on a curve drawn from the same numbers that shape the sound.

  • sustain is a level, release is a time
  • pluck vs. pad vs. organ presets
  • live stage indicator
OPEN LAB → EXPERIMENT 004

FM Synthesis Lab

Two operators, one idea: let a modulator wobble a carrier's pitch at audio rate and hear bells, brass and metal appear as sidebands in the spectrum.

  • carrier, modulator, ratio, depth
  • fixed vs. ratio modulators
  • the principle behind the DX7
OPEN LAB → EXPERIMENT 005

Harmonics Lab

Build a sound from eight sine waves by hand. Draw a sawtooth, a square or something new, and see the recipe appear as peaks in the spectrum.

  • additive synthesis, eight harmonics
  • theoretical sum vs. measured wave
  • the missing fundamental
OPEN LAB → EXPERIMENT 006

Beats and Tuning Lab

Two tones a few hertz apart pulse against each other. Count the beats, tune them to silence, then push them into roughness and a new pitch.

  • beat frequency = the difference
  • tuning by ear, detune for width
  • live peak-level envelope
OPEN LAB → EXPERIMENT 007

Echo and Reverb Lab

Pluck a note into a delay line and a synthetic room. Watch echoes stack up as feedback rises and a reverb tail smear them into space.

  • delay time, feedback, mix
  • impulse-response reverb size
  • rolling level history
OPEN LAB → EXPERIMENT 008

Modulation (LFO) Lab

One slow oscillator, three destinations. Aim it at pitch, loudness or a filter and hear vibrato, tremolo and wah come from the same wobble.

  • rate, depth, shape
  • vibrato vs. tremolo vs. wah
  • when an LFO becomes a tone
