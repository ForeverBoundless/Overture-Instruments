WHY IT EXISTS

THE IDEA

Why Overture exists

The instruments that inspired me are extraordinary. Access to them, though, heavily depends on what I can afford.

I started OI with a dream of being able to explore as many of these sounds as possible while needing to own almost no real hardware. Overture functions as a repository for the resulting MIDI-playable emulators and creative tools I've made. The code is also available for anyone to use, inspect, contribute to, and learn from. That means you, too.

No one can replace the experience of playing a real instrument. But I hope that from Overture you can find new ways to experiment, learn, compose, and find your own unique voice without cost-related barriers.

MEET THE FOUNDER

Hi everyone!
I'm Michael.

I'm a musician and developer building Overture Instruments around three of my favourite things: music, engineering, and figuring out how to get stuff for free.

As someone who lacks the budget for professional sound equipment, I originally created OI as a way to access such tools without breaking the bank. I planned to have Overture up and running in a day or less, structuring it as a simple MIDI-driven audio engine. But somewhere along the way, I realized that I had stumbled upon a great long-term project that could make a difference for my fellow creatives, too.

But Overture is, above all, a passion project. I want to share my knowledge, keep learning, and make something that other musicians can appreciate too. And don't worry: OI's an ongoing project, and there is plenty more to figure out.
