A LITTLE ABOUT OVERTURE

Built by curiosity.
Open to everyone.

Overture Instruments is an independent project exploring what happens when a love of music meets a love of building things.

The goal is simple: make expressive instruments and creative tools easier to explore, without putting them behind a price tag.

01WHY IT EXISTS

THE IDEA

Why Overture exists

The instruments that inspire us can be extraordinary. Access to them, though, often depends on what we can afford.

I started OI with a vision of people being able to explore as many sounds as possible while needing to own almost no hardware. The project builds detailed, MIDI-playable emulators and creative tools with the code available for anyone to use, inspect, learn from, and contribute to.

No one can replace the experience of playing a real instrument. But I hope that from Overture you can find new ways to experiment, learn, compose, and find your own unique voice without the barriers of cost or complexity.

02THE PERSON BEHIND THE PROJECT

MEET THE FOUNDER

Hi everyone!
I'mMichael.

I'm a musician and developer building Overture Instruments around two things I never get tired of: making music and figuring out how things work.

Music gives me the questions. Building gives me a way to chase them. I started out learning through Python and Java, then moved into the web technologies that let me create playable instruments directly in a browser, with custom audio engines and MIDI support.

Overture is a place to bring those interests together, keep learning in public, and make something that other musicians can use. It's an ongoing project, and there is plenty more to figure out.
